How Payment Hold Works

  • • Payments are confirmed and held for 1 day
  • • After 1 day, they become available for release
  • • You have 7 days to request the release
  • • Platform fee (~5%) is deducted from each payment
  • • You receive the net amount to your verified account

Tips for Faster Payments

  • • Verify your payment details in account settings
  • • Use the "Request Release" button to notify operator
  • • Include your land and reservation details in requests
  • • Check communication history for payment status
  • • Contact support if payment is overdue
